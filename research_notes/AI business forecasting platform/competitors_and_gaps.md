# Competitive Landscape: AI Business Forecasting, FP&A, Demand Planning and Decision Analysis for SMBs (Global + Egypt/MENA), as of 2026-10-26

> **Method note for the report writer.** Research ran on 2026-10-26. Almost every direct page fetch (vendor pricing pages such as fathomhq.com, floatapp.com, brixx.com, jirav.com, wafeq.com, and aggregators such as cfoshortlist.com, getaleph.com, thefinanceweekly.com, fortune.com) failed with DNS errors. Most figures below therefore come from **search-engine summaries of third-party pages** (affiliate roundups, vendor-written comparisons, pricing trackers), not from verified vendor price sheets. Where one search returned several URLs and the summary did not say which page held which number, I cite the most likely page or group the pages together and say so. **Treat every price as indicative and flag it as "needs verification" in the final report.** Also note a name collision: searches for "Runway pricing" return **Runway ML (AI video)**, not **Runway Financial (FP&A)**. Its $12–$95/seat figures are not FP&A prices.

## 1. FP&A / planning platforms: SMB fit and pricing in 2026

### Takeaway
The market splits into three price bands. (a) Enterprise and upper mid-market platforms (Anaplan, Pigment, Workday Adaptive, Planful, OneStream) cost about $25K–$115K+ per year, quote-only, plus implementation. (b) Mid-market "modern FP&A" tools (Cube, Jirav, Drivetrain, Abacum, Runway, Datarails) start at about $10K–$45K per year. (c) SMB and accountant-channel reporting and cash-flow tools (Fathom, Futrli, Float, LivePlan, Brixx) cost about $15–$720 per month. Only band (c) is really affordable for small businesses. Those tools are mostly English-language, tied to Xero/QuickBooks, focused on reporting and cash flow, and light on AI and external market data.

## 2. AI data-analysis assistants that already do "upload data, get a forecast"

### Takeaway
General-purpose assistants (ChatGPT, Claude including Claude for Excel, Microsoft Copilot in Excel, Power BI Copilot, Julius) are cheap ($0–$45 per user per month, or about $263/mo and up for Power BI Copilot capacity) and can produce forecasts on demand. The evidence suggests LLMs themselves are weak at numeric time-series forecasting, and spreadsheet benchmarks show significant numerical-accuracy and reliability failures. What these assistants lack is reliability, data prep and cleaning for messy SMB data, external market context, and framing of the business decision. They are also self-serve, not done-for-you.

### Cited Findings
- **Research is skeptical of LLM-native forecasting.** A NeurIPS 2024 paper concluded "it is hard to conclude that LLMs are effective in time series forecasting". Shuffling the input series caused no appreciable change in performance, which suggests the models were not really using temporal structure. — [NeurIPS 2024, "Are Language Models Actually Useful for Time Series Forecasting?"](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/6ed5bf446f59e2c6646d23058c86424b-Paper-Conference.pdf)
- LLMs do better on data with clear periods and trends, and performance falls as the number of periods increases. — [arXiv 2402.10835](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.10835v1)
- **WorkstreamBench (2026)**, which covers end-to-end spreadsheet tasks in finance, found the Claude agent in a commercial GUI performed best, followed by ChatGPT Pro and the OpenAI and Anthropic Excel plug-ins. Failures included **hardcoded values and incomplete outputs** on harder tasks. — [arXiv 2605.22664](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22664v1)
- On the **Vals.ai Excel Modeling Benchmark**, numerical accuracy is the bottleneck: the leading model passes only **64% of numerical checks**. Some model names on the leaderboard could not be verified. — [Vals.ai EMB](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb)
- TemporalBench (2026) is a new benchmark for contextual, event-informed time-series tasks. This is the "external context" problem, and its existence shows it is an open research area. — [arXiv 2602.13272](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.13272)
- In an informal practitioner test of building Excel forecasts, Claude ranked first, Copilot second and ChatGPT last. Copilot's formulas had the fewest errors but were the most simplistic (for example, Excel's built-in FORECAST.LINEAR). This is anecdotal and not rigorous. — [F9 Finance](https://www.f9finance.com/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-copilot-for-excel/)
- **Claude for Excel** launched in late Oct 2025 as a beta research preview for Max, Team and Enterprise users. It runs as an Excel sidebar that reads, modifies and builds workbooks while preserving formula dependencies. It came with pre-built finance skills (including DCF) and live-data connectors (LSEG market data, Moody's, Aiera). Its 2026 general-availability status was not confirmed. — [Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/news/advancing-claude-for-financial-services); [TechRadar](https://techradar.com/pro/claude-is-coming-to-your-spreadsheets-but-is-it-enough-to-make-you-an-emperor-of-excel-and-what-will-microsoft-copilot-think)
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot Business** (organizations with 300 or fewer employees) costs **$21/user/mo** as an add-on to a qualifying M365 plan. There was a launch promo of $18 that has since expired. — [Office Watch](https://office-watch.com/2025/microsoft-365-copilot-business-ai-add-on/); [eesel Copilot pricing](https://eesel.ai/blog/copilot-pricing)
- **Power BI Copilot** needs paid Fabric (F) or Premium (P) capacity, not free or trial capacity. The entry **F2 is about $262.80/mo** and small SKUs get throttled. Broader use lands at **F32 (about $4,204.80/mo) or F64 (about $8,409.60/mo)**. Power BI Pro is $14/user/mo and PPU is $24. — [Querio](https://querio.ai/articles/power-bi-pricing-copilot-costs-nobody-mentions); [Casrai](https://www.casrai.org/guides/power-bi-pricing); [Microsoft Fabric blog](https://blog.fabric.microsoft.com/blog/announcing-fabric-copilot-pricing-2/)
- **Julius AI:** sources conflict. One lists Free (15 messages/mo), **Plus $35/mo and Pro $45/member/mo**, and another gives up to **$70/member/mo**. It offers "predictive forecasting with clear charts and confidence intervals", a claim from a review that has not been independently verified. — [eesel Julius pricing](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/julius-ai-pricing); [Querio](https://querio.ai/articles/how-much-is-julius-ai)
- Vendors are exposing their data to general assistants rather than competing with them. Pigment and Wafeq both offer MCP servers so that Claude or ChatGPT can query their data. — [Pigment](https://www.pigment.com/); [Wafeq AI](https://www.wafeq.com/en/wafeq-ai)

### Inferences
- "Upload a CSV and ask for a forecast" is commoditized and nearly free. A solo founder cannot win on that feature alone.
- Defensible value sits in what the assistants miss: (1) cleaning messy SMB exports (Daftra, Odoo, Excel, POS) into a usable series; (2) using proper statistical or ML forecasting (seasonality, Ramadan and Eid effects, FX shocks) rather than an LLM guessing numbers; (3) adding external context such as Egyptian inflation, the EGP rate, CBE rates and sector demand; (4) framing the decision ("should I reorder, hire, raise prices?"); and (5) a human-checked deliverable the owner can trust.
- The LLM is best used as the narrator and interface, layered on a classical or foundation time-series model, rather than as the forecaster.

### Gaps
- No rigorous head-to-head forecast-accuracy test (MAE/MAPE against baselines) of ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini on SMB business data was found.
- Gemini in Google Sheets, ChatGPT's current data-analysis and agent features, Tableau/Salesforce, ThoughtSpot and Zoho Analytics/Zia were not covered by retrieved sources, so there is no 2026 pricing or feature evidence for them.
- Arabic-language performance of these assistants on business analysis was not found.

## 3. Demand/inventory forecasting for SMB retail and e-commerce, and marketing mix modeling (MMM)

### Takeaway
SMB e-commerce demand forecasting is crowded, cheap, and tied to Shopify: about $49–$300/mo for Prediko, Cogsy, Monocle and ReplenishRadar, and about $245–$900+/mo for Inventory Planner and Netstock. Shopify retired its free Stocky app in 2026, which opened room for third-party apps. MMM is open source (Google Meridian, Meta Robyn) but costly to operate. Managed MMM costs about $2K–$5K+ per month and targets brands with $1M+ marketing budgets, which is well above Egyptian SMB means.

### Gaps

## 4. Fractional CFO / FP&A services and freelance marketplaces

### Takeaway
"Done-for-you" forecasting exists mainly as human services. Fractional CFOs charge about $3K–$12K/mo and up in Western markets. Freelance financial modelers charge about $35–$225/hr, or about $1.5K–$5K per three-statement model. A new tier of "AI CFO" apps sells at about $49–$500/mo, but these are US/UK-centric, English-only, and built on QuickBooks/Xero. Accounting incumbents (Intuit, Xero) and Mastercard are bundling AI cash-flow forecasting directly into SMB tools.

### Cited Findings
- **Fractional CFO retainers in 2026:** mostly **$3,000–$12,000/mo**, with outliers up to $20K+. Founder-led mid-market engagements cluster at **$5K–$10K**. — [EightX](https://eightx.co/blog/fractional-cfo-cost-pricing-guide); [Eagle Rock CFO](https://eaglerockcfo.com/blog/research/fractional-cfo-industry-report); [MB Accounting Group](https://mbaccountinggroup.com/fractional-cfo-cost-small-business/)
- EightX prices by company revenue: **under $3M is $3,000–$4,500/mo; $3–10M is $5,000–$8,000; $10–50M is $8,000–$12,000; $50M+ is $15,000–$20,000+**. Hourly rates run **$150–$400**, and one source reports a senior average of **$177/hr**. — [EightX](https://eightx.co/blog/fractional-cfo-cost-pricing-guide); [StartupCFO.ai](https://www.startupcfo.ai/fractional-cfo-cost); [Leaders Adapt](https://www.leadersadapt.com/fractional-cfo-cost/)
- **Upwork:** US cash-flow modeling freelancers typically charge **$75–$148/hr**. An older Upwork guide gives financial modelers at about **$35/hr beginner, $81 intermediate and $225 advanced**. Another source gives **$40/hr offshore to $200/hr** for US CFA or ex-investment-banking modelers. — [Upwork cash flow modeling](https://www.upwork.com/hire/cash-flow-modeling-freelancers/us/); [Upwork/oDesk financial modelers](https://www.odesk.com/hire/financial-modelers/); [goLance rate guide](https://golance.com/hiring/best-freelance-financial-analysts-hourly-rate)

## 7. Where the gaps are, and whether "AI forecast + market context + decision recommendations, done for you" already exists

### Takeaway
Every piece of the proposed offering exists somewhere: AI forecasts (assistants, AI CFO apps), market context (enterprise data connectors), decision advice (fractional CFOs), and Arabic forecasting software (Kudwa in Wafeq). The **combination**, sold as a done-for-you recurring deliverable, in Arabic, priced in EGP for Egyptian micro and small businesses and including local macro context, was **not found**. The globally crowded spots are Shopify inventory apps, Xero/QuickBooks cash-flow tools and mid-market FP&A. The open spots are language and region, price point, deliverable format, and SMB-scale MMM.

### Cited Findings (evidence supporting each gap)
- **Price gap:** SMB tools run about $15–$720/mo (Fathom, LivePlan, Float, Futrli), modern FP&A about $10K–$45K+/yr, and human fractional CFOs about $3K–$12K/mo. — [Cube blog](https://www.cubesoftware.com/blog/best-fpa-software-for-small-businesses); [CheckThat.ai LivePlan](https://checkthat.ai/brands/liveplan/pricing); [EightX](https://eightx.co/blog/fractional-cfo-cost-pricing-guide)
- **Geography and integration gap:** Cube reportedly sells only in the US and Canada, Zeni is not suited outside the US, and most SMB tools integrate with QuickBooks, Xero or FreeAgent. MENA accounting tools are compliance-led (ZATCA, ETA). — [Clockwork / FrontDeskReview roundups](https://frontdeskreview.com/best/fp-and-a-software/); [AI Board Studio](https://ai-board.studio/en/blog/best-ai-tools-for-cfos-2026); [Melio](https://melio.com/blog/10-apps-to-help-you-predict-your-cash-flow/); [Lkwjd](https://lkwjd.com/vat-compliance-software-saudi-smes)
- **Language gap:** the only Arabic AI-forecasting offer found was Kudwa inside Wafeq. An Arabic-language search found no SMB AI sales-forecasting tool for Egypt or KSA. — [Wafeq press release](https://www.wafeq.com/en-eg/press-releases/media-center/wafeq's-partnership-with-kudwa); [SAP MENA Arabic](https://www.sap.com/mena-ar/resources/how-ai-redefines-sales-forecasting)
- **Reliability and context gap in DIY AI:** LLM forecasting is weak (NeurIPS 2024). Spreadsheet agents hardcode values and leave outputs incomplete (WorkstreamBench). Numerical checks pass only 64% of the time (Vals.ai). Contextual, event-informed forecasting is still an open benchmark area (TemporalBench). — [NeurIPS](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/6ed5bf446f59e2c6646d23058c86424b-Paper-Conference.pdf); [WorkstreamBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22664v1); [Vals.ai](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb); [TemporalBench](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.13272)

- **Startup-modeling gap after the exits** (Causal, Finmark, Mosaic), as argued by one 2026 blog. — [Burncast](https://blog.burncast.io/posts/startup-financial-modeling-gap/)
- **Macro-volatility need in Egypt:** FX moved from about 25 to about 48 EGP/USD (2022–24), inflation peaked at 36%, and SMEs rely on traditional accounting tools. — [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/sme-and-entrepreneurship-policy-in-egypt_195d9c43-en/full-report/the-business-environment-for-smes-and-entrepreneurship-in-egypt_cb33b535.html); [EKB](https://atasu.journals.ekb.eg/article_37839.html)

### Inferences
**Crowded segments to avoid competing in head-on:**
- Horizontal FP&A software (well funded, consolidating).
- Shopify inventory forecasting (more than 10 apps at $49–$300/mo).
- US/UK cash-flow forecasting for Xero/QuickBooks users (incumbents are bundling it).
- Restaurant BI in MENA (Foodics).
- Generic "upload a CSV and ask" analysis (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Julius).

**Plausible niches for a zero-capital solo founder in Egypt:**
1. **Done-for-you monthly "forecast + decision memo" in Arabic** for Egyptian SMEs with about 10–100 employees in distribution, light manufacturing, retail chains, clinics or e-commerce outside Shopify. It would add EGP, inflation, CBE-rate and seasonal context (Ramadan, Eid, back-to-school). The price point would sit between DIY AI apps and a fractional CFO. No direct competitor was found.
2. **Partner or channel play:** an analytics add-on service resold through Daftra (Egyptian, no AI forecasting found), Odoo partners in Egypt (who sell EGP 125K+ projects) or accounting firms. The founder would supply the forecasting and advisory layer, and the partner the customers and data.
3. **Export the service to Western SMBs** through Upwork or Fiverr, priced below US fractional CFOs, with AI providing the leverage. This competes with offshore modelers at $35–$80/hr, so differentiation on decision framing is needed.
4. **SMB-scale MMM or budget-allocation reports** on Meridian or Robyn for brands spending roughly $5K–$50K/mo on ads. That is below Recast and Cassandra's floors.

**Competitive threats to watch:**
- Kudwa and Wafeq extending to smaller firms or to Egypt.
- Daftra or Qoyod adding native AI forecasting.
- Microsoft Copilot and Claude for Excel improving reliability.
- Foodics expanding beyond restaurants.

**Deliverable format gap:** almost all offerings are software (self-serve dashboards) or bespoke consulting. Few sell a fixed-scope, recurring report product, which fits a no-capital founder (low build cost, cash-generating, and validates demand before any software is built).

### Gaps
- Willingness to pay among Egyptian SMEs for forecasting or advisory (EGP price points) was not found and needs primary research such as interviews or landing-page tests.
- Kudwa's pricing, and whether its Arabic forecasting via Wafeq is live and used by Egyptian customers, could not be confirmed.
- No data was found on whether Egyptian accounting firms or Big 4 offer packaged SME forecasting, or what local fractional CFOs charge.
- Pricing could not be verified against vendor pages because of fetch failures. All figures need re-checking before publication.
